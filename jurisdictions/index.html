EARTH

Applies to
Reception on Earth, or within the light second of it: Qicha Station and the Chadu relays included. Reception at the Moon’s stations, Shackleton and Peary, by the Register’s direction of 1 November 2066.
On the record
Earth: 3WC, ASP Art. 9.Form OWP-3, JURISDICTION
Forum
The presence courts.ASP Art. 41
Contest
Statements attributed to anchored persons, by the deadline that each sending record states.ASP Arts. 36, 37
Replies
Lodged at any relay of the carrier that carried the sheet, or at a reply desk; forwarded unacknowledged, in the next window.OWP §5(1)–(3)
Desks
On Earth and in Earth orbit.

MARS

Applies to
Reception on Mars or a moon of Mars: Bocha, Stickney, Arcadia, Jezero and Valles stations included, and vessels berthed there.
On the record
Mars: MLC rules until the Earth–Mars mutual recognition schedule covers anchorings.Form OWP-3, JURISDICTION
Forum
The Mars Local Courts.ASP Art. 51
Replies
Held for a sender in transit at the station of destination, and released on arrival.OWP §5(3)(b)
Desks
The desk at Bocha Station, and agents at Arcadia, Jezero and Valles.

IN TRANSIT

Applies to
Reception aboard a vessel in transit, from its unberthing to its arrival.
On the record
In transit: the master’s court.Form OWP-3, JURISDICTION
Forum
The master’s court, held by the captain aboard.ASP Art. 52
Provisions
The carrier’s prevail as to carriage, and the vessel’s as to conduct aboard.OWP §8(3)
Replies
Held at the station of destination, or aboard in a sealed store that opens at arrival.OWP §5(3)(b)

OATH ZONES

Applies to
Reception in a collector-free zone designated under ASP Art. 46. OATH zones are the commonest.
On the record
OATH zones: passive-receipt exemption, collectors off.Form OWP-3, JURISDICTION
Receiving
With every collector that the receiver carries or controls switched off.ASP Art. 32
Directory
Kept by local bodies, on Fallow.
