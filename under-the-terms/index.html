SENDERS OF RECORD

Entering the class
The Register certifies a channel on the application of its Sender of record, on Form OWP-2, once the Sender has undertaken the Terms and its Carrier has accepted them.OWP §3(1)
Reading back
A Sender shall not read back while in transit. No Sender shall collect, or cause or permit another to collect, data concerning Receivers.OWP §2(1), (4)(a)
Acknowledgement
No Sender shall request an Acknowledgement, or make an Acknowledgement a condition of carriage.OWP §2(4)(c)
Selection
No Sender shall select, order or alter a Publication by reference to a Count or other Read-back.OWP §2(4)(b)
Machines
A machine shall not be a Sender of record.OWP §3(2); MC §2

CARRIERS

Carriage
Each Sheet, complete and unaltered, with Copy B of its sending record, in the Link Window open at the time of sending or, if none is open, in the next window.OWP §5(5)(a)
Replies
Forwarded unacknowledged, in the order lodged and without selection.OWP §5(2), (3)
Routing metadata
Kept for the statutory minimum retention and for no longer; disclosed to the Sender only as the Sender’s own times of sending.OWP §2(2)
Loss
Traffic lost in carriage is not resent, and a Sender in transit is not told of it.OWP §5(6)
Provisions
Each Carrier publishes its in-transit provisions.OWP §§5(5)(f), 8(1)

STREAMS

A Stream
A service that receives Publications in order to show them to others.OWP §1(1)
Counts
No Count of a Publication of the class is shown. Where one would stand, the rest figure –––.OWP §6(1), (3)
Order
The Sheets of a channel of the class are not selected or ordered by reference to a Count.OWP §6(5)
The card
Shown wherever the channel is shown.OWP §3(3)
Acceptance
A Stream that shows a Publication of the class, its card or the class mark accepts the two rules above, on Counts and on order, for that Publication.OWP §6(2)

RECEIVERS

Replying
Any person may lodge a Reply to a Sheet at any Relay of the Carrier that carried the Sheet. A Reply lodged at one Relay is lodged at all of the Carrier’s Relays. Reply desks.OWP §5(1)
After lodging
No Carrier, Relay, Register or Sender will acknowledge the Reply, confirm its delivery, or say whether it has been read. A lodging number records the lodging, the last event on its record.OWP §5(2)
Standing
A Reply is a clue. Lodging it leaves the year’s LEAF allowance as it was.OWP §5(4)
Where you are
As to reception in any place, the law of that place prevails over the Terms.OWP §10(3)
