Reply Desk Officer.

Take replies at the Register’s desk: check the slip, give a lodging number, and pass each reply on in the order lodged.

THE ROLE

Role number
SIN-2095-012
Team
Reply desks
Place
Singapore, at the Chadu relays’ ground station
Hours
36 a week, in shifts across the relay’s link windows, nights included.
Contract
Permanent.
Pay
Steps 40 to 47 of the Register’s scale, with a shift allowance.The Register’s scale is indexed to the median wage of the place of work.

THE WORK

  • Receive replies on Form OWP-4, or in any other form that the carrier accepts.
  • Give each reply a lodging number, which records the lodging.
  • Pass replies to the carrier in the order lodged.
  • Keep the desk’s queue and its published hours.

REQUIREMENTS

  • The right to work at the place of work.
  • English, and one other language of the region.
  • Experience at a counter.

PREFERRED

  • Shift work at a carrier or a station.

TERMS

  • Five weeks’ leave a year.
  • Medical cover.
