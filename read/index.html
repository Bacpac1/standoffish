MLC Mars Local Courts MLC rules: the rules they apply OWP §1(1) —

Grade code G O-D/J (ASP Art. 9(9)). G: A anchored · P present · C clue. O (origin): 1 in person · 2 recorded · 3 remote · 4 machine-made; where more than one applies, the highest. A and P take origin 1 only. G and O may be written alone ("C4"). D (delay class, light-time from the place made to the centre of the Earth): W under 10 s · L 10 s or more, under 180 s · D 180 s or more. J (where made): E Earth or within the light second · M Mars or a moon of Mars · T vessel in transit · S other station. NOSLEN 01: C4-D/T. A Sheet sent on VD 1 of the same voyage (DAS 1.4): C4-W/T.

Source class M n X (MC §5(3)). n (host): 1 fixed installation · 2 personal terminal · 3 vessel or system · 4 autonomous body. X (residence): R resident · T transient · S service. Terminal-resident = M2R. NOSLEN: M2R; the FUCHA's own system: M3R.

Check digit of an MR number (MC §5(2), Schedule 2: mod 10). 1. Write the 12 digits. 2. From the rightmost digit leftward, double the 1st, 3rd, 5th, 7th, 9th and 11th digits. 3. From any doubled value above 9, subtract 9. 4. Add the 12 values. 5. The check digit is the amount that brings the sum to the next multiple of 10 (0 if the sum is a multiple of 10).

Sum 63; the next multiple of 10 is 70; check digit 7: MR 680977074986-7. To verify a complete number, count the check digit as the 1st from the right and double the 2nd, 4th … 12th; the sum (63 + 7 = 70) is a multiple of 10.
