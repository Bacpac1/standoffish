RD/2095/002 · 14 APRIL 2095

Subject
The class mark on goods for sale
Question
A certified channel asked to print the class mark on a series of garments for sale.
Determination
The class mark belongs on the forms the Terms give it: the card, the Sheets and the sending records of certified channels. The Register answered with those forms and entered the request and the answer in the channel's mark file; the channel printed the garments with its own name.OWP §7(2), (3)

Current.

The class mark.

RD/2094/005 · 22 NOVEMBER 2094

Subject
Receivers by channel
Question
A certified channel asked for the number of persons who had received its Sheets in 2093.
Determination
The Register keeps two figures a year, the channels in the class and the Sheets sent, for the class and by place of sending. It answered with the annual statement for 2093 and closed the request with a reference to OWP §6(4)(b).OWP §§2(3), 6(4)(b)

RD/2092/003 · 9 MAY 2092

Subject
A caption error
Question
A channel reported an error in the captions of a Sheet already sent.
Determination
The examiner compared the Register's copy with the carrier file. An error made in the Register's copy is corrected there, dated, with the copy as received kept beside it. An error in the Sheet as sent stands with the Sheet, and the channel may send a new Sheet with the corrected captions, whose sending record names the Sheet it corrects. The error was in the Sheet, and the channel sent a correction.OWP §3(6), (7), (9)
Status
Current.
Practice
Corrections and appeals.

RD/2090/003 · 16 OCTOBER 2090

Subject
Arrival at another station
Question
A vessel bound for Bocha Station first berthed at Stickney Station, on Phobos; replies for the senders aboard were held at the desk at Bocha.
Determination
Arrival is the first berthing. The Register released the held replies to Stickney Station, and the senders' periods to answer ran from that berthing. The revision of 2091 wrote the rule into the definition of Arrival.OWP §§5(3)(b), 9(3) (r2086); §1(1), "Arrival" (r2091)

RD/2089/004 · 7 FEBRUARY 2089

Subject
A Sheet lost between relays
Question
A Sheet was lost in a handover between relays, and its sender asked for it to be sent again.
Determination
Traffic lost in carriage stays lost. The carrier entered the loss in its routing metadata, with the window and the custody references, and kept its carrier file; the Register publishes the sending record as received. A claim goes to the carrier of record, up to its liability limit, and the sender may send a new Sheet with its own sending record.OWP §§3(7), 5(6), 8(1)(f); Schedule B
Status
Current.
Practice
Carriers.

RD/2089/001 · 20 JANUARY 2089

Subject
Reconsideration of RD/2088/006
Question
The channel asked for RD/2088/006, the suspension of its certification for misuse of the class mark, to be reconsidered.
Determination
An officer apart from the first decision reconsidered it, on the Stream's records that the channel supplied. The ranked list was the Stream's own page, generated by the Stream; the channel's card, Sheets and sending records carried the mark as the Terms give it. RD/2088/006 is set aside, and the channel's certification stands as before; the suspension and the review stay in the channel's file. The Stream's display went to the Register as a matter of its own, and the Stream took the mark down on the Register's request.OWP §§7(3), 9(1)(b), (2) (r2086); the Register's procedure for reconsideration

RD/2087/001 · 12 MARCH 2087

Subject
A reply lodged with another carrier
Question
A reply to a Sheet carried by the Chadu relays was lodged at a relay of the Deimos relays.
Determination
The desk took the reply and gave its lodging number. It recorded the Sheet's carrier and the route onward, and the reply went to the Chadu relays over the trunk in the order lodged, the time and custody of the handover entered against the lodging number. The Sheet keeps its carrier of record.OWP §5(1)–(3) (r2086)

RD/2086/002 · 23 AUGUST 2086

Subject
Delivery receipts
Question
A carrier offered senders, for a fee, a receipt confirming that their Sheets had been received.
Determination
A receipt of delivery is an Acknowledgement, and the class carries Sheets and Replies one way. The carrier withdrew the receipt from its carriage in the class within 30 days, and its acceptance of the Terms stood. Its other services run under their own terms and names.OWP §1(1), "Acknowledgement"; §§2, 5(2) (r2086)
Status
Current.
Practice
Under the Terms, carriers.

RD/2081/003 · 9 JUNE 2081

Subject
The DAS on a sending record
Question
To what precision a sending record writes the Delay at Sending.
Determination
In whole seconds, rounded to the nearest, as the carrier certifies it; the carrier file keeps the carrier's own value. Records made before the revision of 2091 keep their whole seconds.OWP §3 (r2079); Form OWP-3 (r2074)
Status
Superseded by Revision 2091.
Practice
Reading a record.

RD/2066/001 · 1 NOVEMBER 2066

Subject
The Moon's stations
Question
Which notice a sending record gives for reception at the Moon's stations, 1.19 to 1.36 light-seconds from Earth, where crews serve in rotation.
Determination
The Earth notice. Each station answers to the state that registers it, a party to 3WC. The direction concerns the notices on the record; the courts keep their own jurisdiction.OWP §10(3) (2066); Form OWP-3 (2066), Jurisdiction

Current.
