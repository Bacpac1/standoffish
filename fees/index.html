HOW FEES WORK

The unit
Wage-hours and wage-minutes: the median hourly wage of the place where the fee is paid, as published for the year. The desk states the amount in the money of the place before payment.OWP Schedule B
The schedule
Fixed each year on 1 March, from the audited costs of the year before, to meet those costs. Carriers that have accepted the Terms pay yearly dues agreed with each. A surplus goes into the next year’s schedule.
Fixed
For an application, when it is accepted complete at a desk.
The undertaking
Made through an anchor network, and priced by it. CIVIC’s basic tier is free.
Agents
Charge the Register’s fees for its services. Any service of the host’s own is agreed and priced before it begins.
Need
A fee is waived on a statement of need, made at the counter.
Far and in transit
At Bocha, in the unit of the place. A sender in transit settles on arrival; the fee is the one fixed when the application was accepted.
Refunds
In full when an application is withdrawn before its examination begins.
