Coverage.

Where the carriers of the class reach, and how long the light takes to get there.

We cover everywhere light goes.

The question is when. Each place below is given with its one-way delay and its delay class, as the Gazetteer has them; a carrier adds its next window on top.

HOW TO READ IT

Delay
One way, at the moment of sending. A reply takes as long again.ASP Art. 2
Class
W under 10 s. L from 10 s to under 180 s. D at 180 s and beyond. It is the letter after the dash in every grade code: Reading a record.
The readout
Top right of every frame: the light-time when the frame was made.OWP §4(8)
Your own
A Sheet’s delay is written on its sending record, as its DAS, and certified by its carrier on request.OWP §§3(5), 5(5)(b)
