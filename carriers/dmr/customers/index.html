The Deimos relays · Terms of carriage.

For anyone sending from Mars, and anyone on Earth waiting to hear from it.

Welcome to the far end.

These are our terms of carriage, as the questions asked at the desk at Bocha Station. They sit under the One-Way Publication Terms and beside our in-transit provisions, edition 2095.1; where a line here and a line there differ, the line there is the term. Sections marked * outlast the voyage. Sending from Earth or from a vessel? The Chadu relays’ terms.

WHO IS MY CARRIER?

Whoever heard you first.
A Sheet’s Carrier of record is the carrier whose relay first receives it: us, for Sheets sent at Mars; the Chadu relays, for Sheets sent on Earth, within the light second, at the Moon’s stations and aboard the vessels they hold windows for. The Carrier of record keeps the file and answers for the Sheet. The other one carries it.Provisions, Interline carriage; OWP §3(7)

HOW OFTEN CAN I REACH EARTH?

Twelve times a day.
The trunk opens for 2 hours at every even hour, TCB. After that the light takes what it takes.Provisions (a)

WHAT HAPPENS AT CONJUNCTION?*

We stop.
While the Sun stands within 2° of the line between Mars and Earth, the trunk stands down: in 2096, from 24 July to 3 August. Your traffic waits at DMR1 in the order lodged, and goes oldest first when the trunk reopens. THE SUN IS NOT A SERVICE FAILURE. NO CREDIT IS GIVEN FOR IT.Provisions, Conjunction

WHAT GOES FIRST?

Lives. Then weather.
Safety-of-life traffic leads every window and has the distress link at all hours. Dust-storm warnings for the surface stations and for craft in transit come next. Then the stations’ own work. Then you.Provisions (c), (e)

WILL I KNOW IT ARRIVED?

No.
The same answer as on the ring. We forward unacknowledged, and so does every carrier of the class.OWP §§2(1), 5(2)

WHERE ARE MY REPLIES?

At the desk at Bocha.
Replies for a Sender aboard a local craft wait at the Register’s desk at Bocha Station and are released on Arrival, to the station where the craft berths. Replies for a Sender aboard a transfer vessel go to that vessel’s Carrier of record.OWP §5(3)(b); provisions (b)

WHAT IF YOU LOSE MY SHEET?*

10 wage-hours.
OUR LIABILITY FOR THE LOSS, CORRUPTION OR DELAY OF ONE SHEET IS CAPPED AT 10 WAGE-HOURS, AT THE MEDIAN WAGE OF THE PLACE OF LODGING. That is Schedule B’s floor, and we stand on it. Claims go to the Carrier of record.OWP §8(1)(f); Schedule B; provisions (f), Interline carriage

WHERE DO WE ARGUE?*

Here.
At Mars: MLC rules, until the Earth–Mars mutual recognition schedule covers the matter. In transit: the master’s court, aboard.ASP Arts. 9, 52

Mail travels under our mail terms, after operational traffic.Provisions (e)
