UNDER THE TERMS

Accepting the Terms
In writing, at the seat. The Register lists the carrier here from that day, and its relays take replies to the Sheets it carries.OWP §5(1)
Provisions
Each carrier for vessels in transit publishes its windows, the keeping of replies for senders in transit, safety-of-life traffic, its ephemeris, its operational traffic and its liability limit per Sheet.OWP §8(1)
Liability
Each carrier’s limit per Sheet stands at or above Schedule B’s floor, 10 wage-hours.OWP §8(1)(f); Schedule B
