(a) Link Windows

One window each voyage day with each vessel in transit, in both directions: 8 hours for a crewed vessel, 2 hours for a cargo vessel. Windows are scheduled in TCB and numbered LW ddd/nn, by the ordinal day of opening and the order of opening that day across all the relays' windows, about 26 a day: with vessels in transit, with the Deimos relays over the Earth–Mars trunk, and with stations beyond the light second (OWP §1(3)(b)).

A Sheet sent outside its vessel's window goes in the next window (OWP §5(5)(a)).

(b) Replies for Senders in transit

Replies lodged for a Sender aboard a vessel in transit go up in that vessel's next window into the vessel's sealed store, kept under the master's key and opened at Arrival (OWP §5(3)(b)). Each vessel's provisions set out how its store is kept (OWP §8(2)). Where a vessel's provisions provide for holding at the station of destination, replies wait at the Register's desk there and are released on Arrival.

(c) Safety-of-life traffic

Carried first: at the head of every window, ahead of all other traffic, and at all hours on the relays' distress link, which every relay keeps open. A distress call takes over any window in progress.

(d) Ephemeris

The DAS is computed from the relays' ephemeris, edition CE 2095.2, with planetary positions updated each 1 January and 1 July, and each vessel's orbit from its navigation report of the day. The relays certify the DAS and the time of Earth receipt on request; the certified values govern (OWP §§3(5), 5(5)(b)).

(e) Operational traffic

Navigation: tracking, ranging and orbit updates. The vessel's telemetry. Crew health and medical consultation. Cargo manifests. The master's court's notices to the Mars Local Courts and to the presence courts. Machine-registry entries for sources aboard (MC §5). Crew correspondence, under the relays' mail terms. Operational traffic travels in the vessel's window, after safety-of-life traffic.

(f) Liability

The carrier liability limit is 12 wage-hours per Sheet, at the median wage of the place of lodging. OWP Schedule B's floor is 10 wage-hours.

Routing metadata

Kept for the statutory minimum retention, 400 days from sending, then erased (OWP §2(2); ASP Art. 54(3)).

The relays

Six relays, CDX1 to CDX6, on the geostationary ring; ground stations at Santa Maria (Azores), Malindi, Singapore, Wenchang, Honolulu and Kourou. The Register keeps reply desks at the ground stations and at Relays CDX1 and CDX3, and a reply lodged at one is lodged at all (OWP §5(1)).
