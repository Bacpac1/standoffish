The Chadu relays · Terms of carriage.

For Senders, for Receivers, and for anyone lodging a reply.

Thank you for sending with the Chadu relays.

These are our terms of carriage, put as the questions we are asked at the counter. They sit under the One-Way Publication Terms and beside our in-transit provisions, edition 2095.1; where a line here and a line there differ, the line there is the term. Sections marked * outlast the voyage. Sending from Mars? The Deimos relays’ terms.

WHO IS THIS BETWEEN?

You and us.
“You” are a Sender of record, a vessel, a Receiver, or a person lodging a reply. “We” are the Chadu relays. “Traffic” is whatever you hand us to carry: Sheets, replies, and the records that travel with them.OWP §1(1)

HOW FAST IS IT?

At the speed of light.
In vacuum: 299,792,458 metres a second. It is the only speed we offer, and no faster one is for sale. How long that takes depends on where you are. DELAY IS NOT A FAULT, AN OUTAGE, OR A GROUND FOR A CREDIT.
Eventually.
Your vessel has one window each voyage day. A Sheet sent outside it goes in the next. Aboard the FUCHA: its notices.OWP §5(5)(a); provisions (a)

WILL I KNOW IT ARRIVED?

No.
We forward unacknowledged. No delivery receipt, no read receipt, no “seen”, no count of anything. THIS IS THE SERVICE AND NOT A LIMITATION OF IT. If someone offers to tell you who is watching, they are not a carrier of the class.OWP §§2(1), 5(2)

CAN I GET MY REPLIES?

Yes. On arrival.
Replies lodged for a Sender in transit go up in the vessel’s next window, into a sealed store kept under the master’s key. It opens at Arrival. Until then nobody reads them to you, us included.OWP §5(3)(b); provisions (b)

WHAT DOES A REPLY COST?

Nothing.
Lodging a reply is free at any of our relays and at the Register’s reply desks. You get a lodging number. It records the lodging and no later event: it is not a tracking number, and there is nothing to track.OWP §5(1), (2)

IS THERE A CAP?

No. There is a window.
What fits in your window goes in your window. What does not goes in the next. We do not rank traffic by who is watching it, because nobody is counted.
One exception.
Safety-of-life traffic goes first: at the head of every window, and at all hours on the distress link. A distress call takes over any window in progress, yours included.OWP §2(5)(a); provisions (c)

WHERE DOES IT WORK?

Where a relay can see you.
Six relays on the ring, six ground stations, and the Earth–Mars trunk with the Deimos relays. Coverage means line of sight in a scheduled window. While the trunk stands down at solar conjunction, traffic that crosses it waits. SERVICE IS NOT AVAILABLE BEHIND THE SUN.Provisions (a); the Deimos relays’ provisions, Conjunction

WHAT DO YOU KEEP ABOUT ME?

Routing metadata. For 400 days.
The times of sending, lodging, receipt and forwarding; the relays and windows used; the DAS. We keep it for the statutory minimum and no longer, and we never read it back to a Sender in transit. Of a Receiver we keep the address the traffic was delivered to. That is the whole file.OWP §§1(1), 2(2)

WHO SAYS WHAT TIME IT WAS?

We do.
The DAS and the time of Earth receipt are computed from our ephemeris, edition CE 2095.2. We certify both on request. The certified values govern.OWP §§3(5), 5(5)(b); provisions (d)

WHAT IF YOU LOSE MY SHEET?*

12 wage-hours.
OUR LIABILITY FOR THE LOSS, CORRUPTION OR DELAY OF ONE SHEET IS CAPPED AT THE CARRIER LIABILITY LIMIT: 12 WAGE-HOURS A SHEET, AT THE MEDIAN WAGE OF THE PLACE OF LODGING. Schedule B’s floor is 10. We answer for carriage. We do not answer for what a Sheet says, for who receives it, or for the time light takes.OWP §8(1)(f); Schedule B; provisions (f)

WHERE DO WE ARGUE?*

It depends where you are.
On Earth: 3WC, under ASP Art. 9. At Mars: MLC rules, until the Earth–Mars mutual recognition schedule covers the matter. In transit: the master’s court, aboard.ASP Arts. 9, 52

CAN YOU CHANGE THESE TERMS?

Yes. By a new edition.
A new edition is filed with the Register before it takes force. The edition in force when a Sheet was sent is the edition that carried it. Remaining in a relay’s line of sight is not acceptance of anything.OWP §8(1)

ANYTHING ELSE?

Precedence.
OWP Terms ≻ carrier file ≻ these questions. Where our provisions and a vessel’s differ, ours prevail as to carriage and the vessel’s as to conduct aboard.OWP §8(3)
