THE MARK

The figure
A point, and three arcs struck from that point on one side, opening away from it, in the proportions of Schedule A.OWP §7(1)
Kept
By the Register, which entered it in 2079.OWP §7(2); Amendment 3
Used
By certified channels: on the card, the Sheets and their sending records, while certification stands.OWP §7(2)
The lines
With the mark, a channel prints the class line, STANDOFFISH · OWP., and may add the Register’s line, Delivered at the speed of light. Eventually., each as written.OWP §7(5)

SCHEDULE A

The point
A disc of radius 1.
The arcs
Three, centred on the point, each through 180°, at radii 1.96, 3.72 and 5.00.
Strokes
0.42, 0.35 and 0.30, from the inner arc out.
Clear space
5.00 on every side of the figure.
Smallest size
3 mm across the outer arc in print; 12 pixels on a screen.
Colour
One colour, on a ground that gives it a contrast of 4.5 to 1 or more.

MISUSE

What it is
Use for a channel outside the class, or under suspension or withdrawal; use on other matter, or with a service that collects data concerning receivers or shows a Count; altering the figure, by reversing it, closing its arcs, adding an arc or turning one toward the point; joining it to a speaker tag, a witness point, a network mark or a mark of grade; using it for the cross-delay arc.OWP §7(3); ASP Art. 11
What follows
The Register requires the misuse to cease, may publish notice of it, and may suspend or withdraw certification. Presenting the mark as a mark of grade may also be presence fraud.OWP §§7(4), 9; ASP Art. 31
Reports
In writing to the seat, with where and when the mark was seen. Contact.
