Service standards.

How long each service takes, from the moment it is accepted complete.

STANDARDS

Replies
A lodging number at lodging. Forwarded in the next window, in the order lodged.OWP §5(2), (3)
Applications: completeness
Checked within 5 working days; an application that needs more comes back with a list of what it needs.
Applications: decision
Within 20 working days of a complete application; a different date is given in writing where more examination is needed.OWP §3(1)
Copies and true copies
At the seat, the same day. Elsewhere, within 5 working days.
Research in the records
A written quote within 5 working days.
Corrections of the Register’s own error
Within 10 working days.
Correspondence
Answered within 10 working days, by the route it came by where the route allows.
Complaints
A first answer within 5 working days; a decision within 20.

HOW TIME IS COUNTED

The clock
Starts when a complete item is accepted at the first desk, an agent’s included, and runs on when the item is sent on to the seat. It keeps the days and the time scale of that desk: UTC on Earth and on the ring, TCB at Bocha.
Pauses
During carriage between desks and windows, during an appointment with an anchor network, and while the Register waits for information it has asked for. While the Earth–Mars trunk stands down at solar conjunction, work that crosses it waits, and the desks at Mars keep their hours and give lodging numbers: Conjunction.

WHEN A STANDARD IS MISSED

A standard missed
The Register writes with the reason and a new date, and waives the fee for the service where the delay is its own.
Closures
Shown on Lintel, with the nearest open desk.
