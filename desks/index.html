    AT A DESK

    Replies
    Lodged at a reply desk, or at any relay of the carrier that carried the sheet. Each is given a lodging number.OWP §5(1), (2)
    Applications
    Received at the seat and by agents, on Form OWP-2, and examined at the seat. The Sender of record undertakes the Terms in person, before three witnesses, through the network at hand.OWP §3(1)
    Verify an agent
    Before you hand an agent an original, check its number against this list: every agent of the Register is on it. Agents.
