Agents.

Bodies with a public counter that take the Register’s business on its behalf.

APPOINTMENT

Who
A body with a public counter in the place, and at least one person at it trained by the Register in the last 24 months.
Term
Three years, renewed after a review.
Scope
The services and the place stated in its entry on Find a desk.
Number
The code of the nearest airport, and a serial. Each number stays with the agent it was given to.

DUTIES

Applications
Received complete, and sent to the seat in the order received, by the next dispatch.OWP §3(1)
Originals
Kept until the next dispatch, in a record of who held them and when.
Copies
Of cards and sending records, as received.OWP §3(9)
Fees
The Register’s schedule. Fees.
Replies
The agent gives the address of the nearest reply desk.OWP §5(1)
Incidents
Any loss or alteration of a document, and any sight of it beyond those entitled, is reported to the seat at once.

AUDIT AND COVER

Audit
Once a year at the counter, by the Register’s Agents team, and again after any incident.
Cover
Insurance or a bond of at least 1,000 wage-hours, for the documents and fees in its keeping.

SUSPENSION AND ENDING

Suspension
At once, where documents are at risk; with reasons in writing, and a review on request.
Ending
At the end of the term, on 90 days’ notice by either side, or at once for misuse.
What stands
An application received while the authority stood stands. Documents in the agent’s keeping go to the seat.
Listing
An agent whose authority has ended is listed below, with the date on which it ended.
