Form OWP-1 · Channel card.

Issued by the Register on certification, and shown wherever the channel is shown.

Issued by the STANDOFFISH Register under OWP §3(10), revision 2091, in force from 1 March 2091. Codes and notation are the same in every language.

  1. Form OWP-1

Shown wherever the channel is shown (OWP §3(3)); on a Stream it is the channel's About. The card shows no Sender of record, no NPR and no Count. Where it differs from the carrier file or the OWP Terms, they prevail (OWP §10(6)).

A Sender's statement optional; 6 lines or fewer; no legend, mark of grade, Count or claim of witness

D coded line grammar below
E now sending Now sending; the source tag (a source) or the name (a speaker), the channel code in brackets; ·; station of departure (place) → station of destination (place); · VD0 and the date of departure; for a vessel bound for Mars, · MOI and its expected date, otherwise · due and the expected date of Arrival; stations by the short name and place that the Register's gazetteer gives

Standard notes (line F). Print each that applies, in this order; renumber when one is omitted.

Note Wording Print where
grade {G O} throughout. Any restatement falls under the restatement-chain step-down rule. (G O completed, as C4) every Sheet of the channel has the same grade and origin (ASP Arts. 9(9), 13)
LND Source tag "{tag}" has passed the label-to-name drift (LND) review threshold; pending review it is not a legal name. an LND review of the source tag is pending (OWP §3(11); MC §5A)
recognition Anchorings made aboard in transit await post-arrival retroactive recognition. the channel sends from a vessel In Transit (ASP Art. 53)
LEAF No annual LEAF allowance drawn; receipt enters no tri-bureau standing score. LEAF 0 for every Sheet of the channel (ASP Art. 2)

Braces in the wording mark an item to complete; they are not printed.

OWP-1 coded line grammar. One space between fields; no field contains a space, except that EFF and its date are separated by one space. In order: 1 OWP1 · 2 vessel or station code, with the place letter J of the place of sending in brackets, FCH(T) · 3 grade code of the channel's most recent Sheet (– before the first) · 4 source class (omitted for a speaker) · 5 MR number (omitted for a speaker) · 6 LEAF + the total drawn for all the channel's Sheets · 7 Carrier code · 8 caption language tags · 9 EFF + effective date.

Notes on the form. (1) EFF is not earlier than the vessel's departure (OWP §3(3)). (2) The Register keeps fields 2, 3 and 6 of the coded line current as the vessel moves and Sheets are sent; that is not a change of particulars. A change of particulars is made on Form OWP-2 and takes effect on a new card with a new EFF. (3) The Register enters the LND note itself (OWP §3(11)) and removes it when the outcome is published.
