  1. Form OWP-2

Made by the Sender of record of a channel, at the seat or with any of the Register's agents (OWP §3(1)); for a change of particulars of a certified channel, by its Sender of record (§3(10)). Fee: 4 wage-hours for an application, 40 wage-minutes for a change of particulars (the Register's schedule of fees). The Register checks the application for completeness, verifies the undertaking with the network that took it, and on certification assigns the channel code and issues the card, Form OWP-1, with its effective date.

No. Field Enter
1 Application certification; or change of particulars and the channel code
2 Channel the name the card's line E will print: a source tag, or the name a speaker consents to show
3 Sender of record the person who undertakes the Terms: the name as anchored, and the anchoring reference
4 Undertaking the network (CIVIC, CONSTANT, MUTUAL or OATH); the record reference; the date, place and time scale of the undertaking (OWP §3(1))
5 Source for a source: source class, MR number, system tag with its date of assignment, and NPR as carrier file (MC §6) or as entered in the registry; for a person: speaker
6 Place of sending vessel or station code and place letter (E, M, T, S); for a vessel, its stations of departure and destination, its date of departure and its expected Arrival
7 Carrier the Carrier code; the Carrier's acceptance of the Terms is on file at the Register
8 Captions the caption language tags, in the order shown (OWP §1(3)(f))
9 Card the Sender's statement for line A, 6 lines or fewer; whether the Register's line follows the class line (§7(5))
10 Correspondence the address for the Register's correspondence about the channel; for a Sender in transit, the vessel's administrative address, through which the Register's notices travel silent on reception (OWP §2(5)(c))
11 Documents each document enclosed, by name
12 Declaration I undertake the Terms for this channel and declare these particulars complete and correct., signed by the Sender of record, or marked as the desk accepts
13 Lodged the desk; the date and time of acceptance, in the desk's time scale

For the Register. Application number · desk · accepted (date, time, scale) · complete on · examining officer · decided on · determination: certified (channel code; card EFF) or refused (reasons in writing; a review may be asked for within 30 days).

Notes on the form. (1) The anchoring record carries the witnesses' particulars; field 4 cites it. (2) A change of particulars takes effect on a new card with a new EFF (Form OWP-1, notes on the form). (3) The fee is fixed when the application is accepted complete.
