Instructions for Form OWP-3.

How to complete a sending record, line by line.

Issued by the STANDOFFISH Register under OWP §3(10), revision 2091, in force from 1 March 2091. Codes and notation are the same in every language.

What's new for r2091

  • The coded line is the first line of Copy B (grammar above).
  • Times are ordinal dates with a named time scale: UTC on Earth and within the light second, TCB beyond.
  • DAS is written in seconds to 0.1 s, with its delay class.
  • The grade code (ASP Art. 9(9)) and the source class (MC §5(3)) are entered.
  • The CONTEST line states the delay-adjusted contest deadline and the value of 2·DAS.
  • The machine legend states the source class and NULL fields.
  • The carrier file prevails over any copy shown (§3(7)); a precedence line closes the record.

General instructions

Purpose of form. Form OWP-3 records the sending of one Sheet. It evidences the sending, not the truth of the content: the certified carrier file is accepted for the times of sending and Earth receipt and for the DAS (ASP Art. 54); no record establishes who or what made the matter sent, or whether it is true, and no record gives a grade (§3(8)).

Who must complete it. The Sender, for every Sheet (§3(4)). Where the speech is a source's, the source's NPR answers for the record (MC §6(6)).

When. At the time of sending. A record is not amended after sending; an error is corrected by a new Sheet whose record identifies the Sheet and the line corrected (§3(6)).

Language and notation. Complete the en form in English. A form in another language has its own wording. Never translate a legend. Codes and notation are identical in every form, and anchor networks are named by their registered names (CIVIC, CONSTANT, MUTUAL, OATH) in all. Do not use angle brackets of any kind; write precedence with ≻ and comparisons with ≥, ≤ or words.

Layout. Each field label is in capitals, followed by two spaces and the value. Separate items within a value with ·. In the wording below, braces mark an item to complete; they are not printed.

Privacy. The Register publishes Copy B in full. Do not enter the name of an NPR who is on file with the Carrier.

Penalties. A false record may lead to suspension or withdrawal of certification (§9). Presenting any frame as witnessed, as P-grade or as A-grade, or removing, obscuring or occluding the phantasm stamp, is presence fraud (ASP Art. 31).

Line by line

Coded line. First line, by the grammar above.

Legends. After the coded line, before SHEET: the clue legend; then, where the speech is a source's, the machine legend with the source class completed. One line each, nothing else on the line, the record's own type and size, no emphasis. Print no legend that does not apply, and never abridge a legend to fit.

SHEET. The Sheet ID; ·; the running time in whole seconds, rounded to the nearest second (147.75 s: 148 s).

SENT FROM. The place of sending (the grade code's origin digit O is the manner of making, not this): the vessel or station code and the terminal (FCH crew terminal); in transit where the vessel was In Transit at the time of sending; any navigational event with which the sending coincided (at departure, at MOI).

SENT. The time of sending as YYYY-DDDThh:mm:ss.s with its scale (TCB beyond the light second, UTC within it), then the voyage day in brackets: 2095-206T14:03:22.4 TCB (VD 120).

DAS. Complete Worksheet A; write A2 with s, then · delay class and the delay class (W, L or D) of the grade code.

Worksheet A: DAS NOSLEN 01
A1 light-time from the place of sending to the centre of the Earth at the time of sending, in seconds, by the ephemeris named in the Carrier's in-transit provisions 328.68
A2 A1 rounded to 0.1 s (exact halves round up) 328.7
A3 delay class: W if A2 is under 10; L if A2 is 10 or more and under 180; D if A2 is 180 or more D

Where the Carrier certifies a different value, the Carrier's value governs (§3(5)); the record is not amended. At the station of departure, before departure, A2 = 0.0.

CARRIER. The Relay that first receives the Sheet (CDX3); the network that licenses the Carrier (CIVIC licence); the Link Window in which the Sheet is forwarded (LW 206/14): the next window if none was open at the time of sending.

SOURCE or SPEAKER. For a source, label the line SOURCE: the source class; the MR number; any system tag, unquoted, with assigned and the VD or date of assignment, then in brackets the VPR values to 2 decimals in the order run, separated by /, and pass ≥ 0.80. A tag is never written as a name. For a person, label the line SPEAKER: the name the person consents to show, or unnamed.

NPR. Sources only. carrier file (MC §6) where the NPR is on file with every Carrier of the Sheet; otherwise the NPR as entered in the registry.

PS. Where any frame is machine-made: the position (TL or TR), every frame, (MC §3), and ; integrity check by the receiver. Otherwise not required (coded PS/NR).

CONTEST. Print on every record, whether or not the Sheet attributes any statement to a person (the receiver judges whether it does): C-grade: n/a. Statements attributed to anchored persons: delay-adjusted contest deadline = t(E,UTC) + 72 h + 2·DAS (here + 72 h {B2}).

Worksheet B: the delay-adjusted contest deadline NOSLEN 01
B1 2 × A2 (double the written DAS; do not recompute from A1; do not round again) 657.4 s
B2 B1 in minutes and seconds 10 min 57.4 s
B3 deadline = t(E,UTC) + 72 h + B1 not on the record

t(E,UTC) is not entered on the record; the Carrier certifies it on request (§5(5)(b)). Example (another Sheet): DAS 192.5, B1 = 385.0 s; t(E,UTC) = 2094-316T08:00:00.0 UTC; deadline = 2094-319T08:06:25.0 UTC. A Sheet sent within the light second has DAS = 0 and a deadline of t(E,UTC) + 72 h (ASP Art. 2).

JURISDICTION. Print in this order: Earth: 3WC, ASP Art. 9. · Mars: MLC rules until the Earth–Mars mutual recognition schedule covers anchorings. · In transit: the master's court. · OATH zones: passive-receipt exemption, collectors off. The Register may prescribe notices for other places. Do not omit a notice because reception there is unlikely.

CARRIER TERMS. Liability per sheet capped at the carrier liability limit. Routing metadata held for the statutory minimum (OWP §2(2)) and never read back to the sender in transit. Replies: any {Carrier code}, forwarded unacknowledged. For a Sender not In Transit, end the second sentence after (OWP §2(2)).

Notes. Numbered: 1. LEAF 0. Where n is above 0: LEAF {n}: {n} signature leaves drawn for statements in this sheet; anchoring records {references}. 2. Presenting any frame as P- or A-grade, or removing or occluding the PS: presence fraud (ASP Art. 31). Where PS is not required: Presenting any frame as P- or A-grade: presence fraud (ASP Art. 31).

Closing line. This record evidences the sending, not the truth of the content. Precedence: OWP Terms ≻ carrier file ≻ this record.
