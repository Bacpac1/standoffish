Notice

The Keepers of the Register have adopted Amendment No. 5 to the One-Way Publication Terms, set out below. It provides for the period to answer where a notice under §9 reaches a Sender after Arrival; for notice to a representative whom a competent court appoints, where contact with a vessel is lost and the court declares a Sender aboard presumed dead; for reconsideration on the Sender's Arrival or reappearance; and for the record of interruptions of carriage.

The Register gives this notice to every Sender of record and to every Carrier under OWP §10(7). Senders In Transit receive it in their vessels' next windows.

The amendment takes effect on 27 January 2096 at 23:59 UTC and applies to notices served from then. For a Sender In Transit on that day, it binds from Arrival. A Sheet stays under the Terms in force when it was sent.

Longyearbyen, 29 October 2095.

The amendment

1. Section 9

After §9(4) insert:

(5) Where a notice under (2) reaches a Sender after Arrival, the period to answer runs from its service.

(6) Where contact with a vessel is lost and a competent court declares a Sender aboard presumed dead, the Register may serve notice under (2) on the representative whom the court appoints to receive it. The representative may answer within 30 days of service, and the Register considers the answer before it decides.

(7) Where certification is suspended or withdrawn after service under (6) and the Sender arrives or reappears, the Sender may ask, within 30 days of Arrival or of verified reappearance, for the decision to be reconsidered by a Keeper or an officer who took no part in it. The Register records the outcome and gives it to the Sender in writing.

(8) The Register records each interruption of carriage that affects the service of a notice under this section, with its known beginning and end and the carrier record relied on. An interruption of carriage is neither Arrival nor service.

2. Commencement

This amendment comes into force on 27 January 2096 at 23:59 UTC. It binds a Sender In Transit on that day from Arrival; a declaration of presumed death is not Arrival.

3. Records

§9(4) continues to apply to Sheets sent before a suspension or withdrawal.
