THE REGISTER

Founded
By engineers of long-haul vessels and far stations, who had published one way, sending, storing and forwarding, since the early 2060s.OWP Preamble
Form
A members’ registry, run at cost. Its pages carry the Register’s own notices alone.
The Terms
Adopted by the founding members on 14 September 2066 and in force from 1 November 2066. Revision 2091 in force from 1 March 2091. The Terms.
Amendment
On 90 days’ notice to every Sender of record and Carrier. An amendment applies to Sheets sent after it takes effect, and binds a Sender in transit from Arrival.OWP §10(7)
Above the Terms
The Three-Witness Convention (2047), the Anchored Speech Protocol and the Machine Clause (2073). Where the Terms conflict with any of them, that instrument prevails.OWP §10(2)
Each year
The number of channels in the class and of Sheets sent in the year, in totals for the class and for each place of sending: the finest division the Register publishes. Annual statement.OWP §6(4)(b)
Suspension
On written notice, with 30 days to answer. Withdrawal of a sender in transit waits for arrival.OWP §9(2)–(4)

MEMBERS

Ordinary Members
Individuals, admitted by the Keepers. Dues: 12 wage-hours a year.
Institutional Members
Bodies, admitted by the Keepers: carriers, houses of the networks, operators of far stations, and the association of long-haul engineers that founded the Register. Dues: 360 wage-hours a year.
Dues
At the seat’s reference wage for the year, each 1 March.
Votes
One for each member.
Apart
Certification, carriage and agency each stand apart from membership; a channel, a carrier or an agent joins as any member does.

THE GENERAL MEETING

When
Each 1 March, at the seat in Longyearbyen.
Notice
The agenda and the accounts, 60 days before.
Business
The audited accounts and the Keepers’ report; the schedule of fees, in force from 1 April; the budget; the election of Keepers; the auditor.
Votes
In person, by a representative, or by post when the ballot reaches the seat by the published closing date. Members at Mars vote by post.
Quorum
One third of the members.

KEEPERS

Five
Elected by the General Meeting for three years, in staggered terms; two consecutive terms at most.
Chief Keeper
Elected by the Keepers each year.
Duties
They appoint the Executive Clerk, approve the operating rules, and keep the Terms, the class mark and the records.
Interests
Declared each year. A Keeper stands aside from decisions on their own carrier, network or agency.

ACCOUNTS AND CONSTITUTION

Basis
At cost. A surplus goes into the next year’s schedule and the reserves. Fees.
Audit
Each year, by the auditor the General Meeting appoints.
Constitution
Changed by two thirds of the votes cast at a General Meeting. The Terms change under their own provision.OWP §10(7)
