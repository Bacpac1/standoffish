The Terms
Adopted by the founding members on 14 September 2066 and in force from 1 November 2066. Revision 2091 in force from 1 March 2091. The Terms.
Amendment
On 90 days’ notice to every Sender of record and Carrier. An amendment does not apply to a Sheet sent before it takes effect, and binds a Sender in transit only from Arrival.OWP §10(7)
Above the Terms
The Three-Witness Convention (2047), the Anchored Speech Protocol and the Machine Clause (2073). Where the Terms conflict with any of them, that instrument prevails.OWP §10(2)
Each year
The number of channels in the class and of Sheets sent in the year, in totals for the class and for each place of sending, and in no finer division.OWP §6(4)(b)
Suspension
On written notice, with 30 days to answer. A sender in transit is not withdrawn before arrival.OWP §9(2)–(4)
