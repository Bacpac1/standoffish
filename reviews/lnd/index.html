Label-to-name drift reviews.

The reviews the Register holds of the tags of certified channels’ sources.

HOW A REVIEW RUNS

Opened
When a source’s tag has been used continuously, in publication or in a register, for 90 days. The Register holds the review for the source of a certified channel.MC §5A(1), (2); Schedule 5
Notice
To the registrar that keeps the source’s entry, on the day the review opens.MC §5A(2)
The card
The Register enters a note on the channel’s card while the review is pending, and removes it when the outcome is published.OWP §3(11); MC §5A(5)
Meanwhile
The tag stays in use, with its pending review shown wherever it appears.MC §5A(4)
Outcomes
(a) the tag stands, with a finding that it is not a legal name; (b) the source is re-tagged and the tag retired; (c) the tag is used under a rotation schedule; (d) its public use is suspended. A review that raises the status of machines goes to the Assembly.MC §§5A(3), 12
Published
Here, with its date, and on the card on the day it takes effect.
