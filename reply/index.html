REPLY DESKS

Where
At any relay of the carrier that carried the sheet, or at the Register’s reply desk at that relay. A reply lodged at one relay is lodged at all of the carrier’s relays. Find a desk.OWP §5(1)
Form
Form OWP-4, or any other form that the carrier accepts.OWP §5(1)
Carriage
Forwarded unacknowledged, every reply, in the order lodged.OWP §5(2), (3)
Lodging number
Records the lodging, the last event on its record.OWP §5(2)
A sender in transit
The reply is held at the station of destination and released on arrival, or held aboard in a sealed store that opens at arrival.OWP §5(3)(b)
Standing
A reply is a clue. Lodging it leaves the year’s LEAF allowance as it was.OWP §5(4)
Fee
Free. Fees.
Hours
The relay’s link windows. Desk hours are published on Lintel.
